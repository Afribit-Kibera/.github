# Afribit Builders

[Afribit Africa](https://afribit.africa) | [Community profile](https://github.com/Afribit-Africa) | [Explore the tools](https://tools.afribit.africa) | [Work with us](https://afribit.africa/contact)

[Projects](#explore-the-work) | [Code & activity](#inside-the-public-code) | [Contributors](#contributors)

We build software around the work of a Bitcoin circular economy: earning from waste collection, paying local businesses, learning Bitcoin and running community tools. Our projects start with the people and constraints of **Kibera, Nairobi**.

## Explore the Work

<!-- catalogue:start -->
### Community & field work

**Taka Sats**
Offline-capable waste-work records, scale and photo evidence, and Lightning payout controls.

[Source](https://github.com/Afribit-Kibera/taka-sats) | [Documentation](https://taka.afribit.africa/docs)

**3 West Satenet**
Portal, payment and network-management tools for a locally operated network. Pilot software; Afribit is not the internet provider.

[Source](https://github.com/Afribit-Kibera/Afribit-Wifi) | [Community portal](https://wifi.afribit.africa)


### Payments & local trade

**Afribitools**
Fastlight Lightning-address validation, clean payment-list exports and batch-payment tools.

[Source](https://github.com/Afribit-Kibera/tools) | [Open app](https://tools.afribit.africa)

**BTCReg**
GPS-first merchant registration, verification and reviewed OpenStreetMap publication for BTC Map discovery.

[Source](https://github.com/Afribit-Kibera/bitmap) | [Open app](https://register.afribit.africa)


### Learning & discovery

**Website & Bitcoin Studio**
Our community website, merchant directory and Bitcoin audio education with synchronised text.

[Source](https://github.com/Afribit-Kibera/Website) | [Bitcoin Studio](https://afribit.africa/studio)

**Afribit AI**
A Bitcoin education assistant shaped for Sheng, Kiswahili and English. Prototype.

[Explore prototype](https://ai.afribit.africa) | Source private


### Team tools

**Afribit Console**
Administration tools for coordinating education sessions, programme records and day-to-day team work.

Source private

**AfriBot for Socials**
Content preparation and social publishing workflows with human approval.

Source private


### Experimental builds

**Afribit Pay**
Pre-launch wallet work based on Blink, exploring Bitcoin and M-Pesa payments. Upstream relationship and credits are retained.

[Source](https://github.com/Afribit-Kibera/afribit-wallet)

**Afribit NFC**
A React Native and backend prototype for NTAG424 contactless card setup and BTCPay Server integrations.

[Source](https://github.com/Afribit-Kibera/nfc)

## Community and Credits

The community runs **3 West Satenet**. Afribit contributes software and technical support. **Kibera Mesh** is research into local calling and chat, not a released communications service.

Our local economic intelligence work is a collaboration with [Insats](https://insats.org). The Bitcoin Studio credits **Btrust Pathways** for the learning material and **Gemini** for AI-generated audio. Afribit Pay retains its **Blink** upstream credits.

## Build With Us

Choose a public project, read its setup and contribution guidance, and use its issues for a focused bug report or proposal. Check the individual licence before reusing code. Keep credentials, payment keys and participant records out of public issues.

For community partnerships, education or deployment support, [contact Afribit](https://afribit.africa/contact).

## Community and Credits

The community runs **3 West Satenet**. Afribit contributes software and technical support. **Kibera Mesh** is research into local calling and chat, not a released communications service.

Our local economic intelligence work is a collaboration with [Insats](https://insats.org). The Bitcoin Studio credits **Btrust Pathways** for the learning material and **Gemini** for AI-generated audio. Afribit Pay retains its **Blink** upstream credits.

## Build With Us

Choose a public project, read its setup and contribution guidance, and use its issues for a focused bug report or proposal. Check the individual licence before reusing code. Keep credentials, payment keys and participant records out of public issues.

For community partnerships, education or deployment support, [contact Afribit](https://afribit.africa/contact).
